Rio Grande do Sul - Brazil

Rio Grande do Sul is the southernmost state in Brazil, sharing borders with Uruguay and Argentina. The region features flat grasslands called pampas in the south and cool mountain forests in the north. Its local culture centers around the traditions of the gaúcho, including drinking chimarrão (traditional mate tea) and eating churrasco (barbecue). The state plays a major role in Brazilian agriculture, livestock raising, and wine production.
